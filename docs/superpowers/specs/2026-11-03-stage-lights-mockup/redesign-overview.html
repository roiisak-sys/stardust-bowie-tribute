What you'd feel

Subtle motion

  • Slow zoom on the opening photo
  • A running ticker line in the brand orange
  • Photos and cards slide in as you reach them
  • The black and white photos turn to colour as they scroll into view (phones have no hover)
  • Everything switches off for people who ask their phone for reduced motion

Photos that open up

  • One tap opens a photo full screen
  • Swipe between photos, pinch to zoom
  • The same for the show posters

No endless lists

  • Past shows sit in a short panel that scrolls on its own, with year chips on top
  • The band is a swipe row and each bio opens in a sheet
  • The "Who was Bowie?" blank stretch becomes a swipe strip of eras

Same content

  • All the text, both languages, stays
  • Hebrew first, right to left
  • The WhatsApp button stays pinned at the bottom
  • Same SEO and page speed rules as today
